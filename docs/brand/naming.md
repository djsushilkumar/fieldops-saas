# FieldOps — Brand Naming Discovery & Evaluation

---

## 1. Executive Context & Legal Disclaimer

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Legal Disclaimer**: The names, candidate concepts, and domain evaluations discussed in this document represent brand exploration and strategic design directions. **None of these names are claimed or guaranteed to be legally available or free of trademark conflicts.** Formal legal trademark screening, common-law clearance, and jurisdiction-specific intellectual property searches must be conducted by certified legal counsel prior to commercial registration or public launch.

The working name **FieldOps** serves as the project codename throughout initial development. This document evaluates the working title and explores strategic naming alternatives for public commercialization.

---

## 2. Naming Evaluation Framework

All candidate names are evaluated against a 9-point criteria framework:

1. **Memorability**: Does the name stick in the mind of a busy field manager or technician?
2. **Pronunciation & Phonetics**: Is it easily spoken over two-way radio, telephone, or noisy dispatch centers without spelling it out?
3. **Professionalism**: Does it inspire confidence when pitching to mid-market enterprise buyers and SMB owners?
4. **Field-Operations Relevance**: Does it convey operational execution, presence, dispatch, or physical delivery?
5. **SaaS Suitability**: Does it feel like a modern, scalable software platform?
6. **International Usability**: Is it easy to pronounce across major languages (English, Spanish, French, German) without unintended pejorative meanings?
7. **Domain Considerations**: Availability or viability of `.com`, `.io`, or sensible prefix/suffix domains (e.g., `get[name].com`, `[name]app.com`).
8. **App Store Suitability**: Does the name fit cleanly beneath an iOS/Android app icon (maximum 11–12 characters to prevent truncation)?
9. **Trademark Risk Profile**: Likelihood of encountering dense trademark clutter in Nice Classes 09 (Software) and 42 (SaaS).

## 4. Strategic Naming Territories & Candidates

### Territory 1: The Direct Operational (Clear, Functional, Grounded)
Names that communicate dispatch, coordination, and physical execution directly.

- **Candidate A: Groundwire**
  - *Rationale*: Evokes electrical grounding, direct connection, and physical infrastructure.
  - *App Label*: "Groundwire" (10 chars).
  - *Domain Viability*: `groundwire.io`, `getgroundwire.com`.
  - *Trademark Profile*: Moderate; frequently used in specialized nonprofit/engineering contexts.
- **Candidate B: SitePulse**
  - *Rationale*: Conveys real-time vitality, continuous monitoring, and physical job sites.
  - *App Label*: "SitePulse" (9 chars).
  - *Domain Viability*: `sitepulse.io`, `sitepulseapp.com`.
  - *Trademark Profile*: High clutter around monitoring software; requires comprehensive search.

### Territory 2: The Verified & Accountable (Proof, Truth, Certainty)
Names emphasizing the verification, auditability, and undeniable record of work.

- **Candidate C: TrueField**
  - *Rationale*: Bridges truth/verification with field execution; communicates unshakeable honesty.
  - *App Label*: "TrueField" (9 chars).
  - *Domain Viability*: `truefield.io`, `gettruefield.com`.
  - *Trademark Profile*: Moderate clutter in agricultural and survey tools.
- **Candidate D: Veriforce / VeriOps**
  - *Rationale*: Blends verification with workforce and operations.
  - *App Label*: "VeriOps" (7 chars).
  - *Domain Viability*: `veriops.com` (likely aftermarket) or `veriops.io`.
  - *Trademark Profile*: Very high trademark overlap (e.g. Veriforce is an existing compliance company).

### Territory 3: The Velocity & Dispatch (Action, Direction, Kinetic)
Names focusing on getting technicians moving, on time, and completing jobs.

- **Candidate E: Waypoint**
  - *Rationale*: A navigational milestone or verified geographic check-in point.
  - *App Label*: "Waypoint" (8 chars).
  - *Domain Viability*: High aftermarket cost for exact match; requires modifiers (`waypointops.com`).
  - *Trademark Profile*: High clutter across GPS and logistics marks.
- **Candidate F: OnSiteGo / OnSiteOps**
  - *Rationale*: Action-first orientation; clearly denotes being on the physical job site.
  - *App Label*: "OnSiteOps" (9 chars).
  - *Domain Viability*: `onsiteops.io`, `getonsiteops.com`.
  - *Trademark Profile*: Moderate; descriptive components.

1. Maintain **FieldOps** as the official engineering codename through Phase 05.
2. Commission an IP attorney to conduct a preliminary knock-out trademark search across Nice Classes 09 and 42 for:
   - `FieldOps`
   - `Groundwire`
   - `TrueField`
   - `SitePulse`
3. Prepare secondary domain registration strategy (`getfieldops.io`, `fieldops.app`) in the event the primary codename is selected for final branding.
