# FieldOps — User Personas

---

## 1. Persona Overview

FieldOps serves five distinct personas across the operational hierarchy of an SMB field organization. Understanding their unique contexts, device constraints, and operational goals is critical to delivering an effective experience.

---

## 2. Persona Profiles

### 2.1. The Business Owner — "David Vance"
- **Role**: Founder / Managing Director / Franchise Owner
- **Company Size**: 15–80 employees across 2–5 local branches
- **Primary Interface**: Web Application (Laptop / Tablet)
- **Primary Goals**:
  - Guarantee contract fulfillment and prevent customer churn due to missed service calls.
  - Maintain operational margins by eliminating ghost hours and unverified billing disputes.
  - Scale the business without adding administrative overhead.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Customers complaining that technicians never showed up, with no data to verify the claim.
  - Blindness to whether teams are operating efficiently or sitting idle.
  - Disjointed tools resulting in lost billable hours and administrative chaos.
- **Daily Workflow**:
  - Morning review of company-wide dashboard (attendance rate, scheduled visits).
  - Mid-day exception review (geofence alerts, overdue emergency jobs).
  - Weekly review of team performance reports and utilization.

---

### 2.2. The Operations Admin — "Elena Rostova"
- **Role**: Operations Administrator / Office Manager
- **Primary Interface**: Web Application (Dual-monitor Desktop)
- **Primary Goals**:
  - Keep employee rosters, client site addresses, and team allocations accurate.
  - Manage subscription tiers, user invitations, and role assignments.
  - Ensure compliance and audit readiness.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Incorrect site coordinates causing workers to check in at wrong locations.
  - Onboarding and offboarding staff manually across multiple spreadsheets.
  - Disputed attendance records requiring hours of manual cross-referencing.
- **Daily Workflow**:
  - Sets up new client locations, verified GPS coordinates, and allowed geofence radii.
  - Onboards new field technicians and assigns them to teams.
  - Exports attendance and task logs at end-of-month.

---

### 2.3. The Operations Manager — "Marcus Chen"
- **Role**: Field Operations Manager
- **Primary Interface**: Web Application (Desktop) + Occasional Tablet
- **Primary Goals**:
  - Balance daily workload evenly across teams and territories.
  - Ensure SLA compliance on critical client service contracts.
  - Identify operational bottlenecks before they cause service delivery failures.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Technicians cherry-picking easy jobs while difficult jobs sit overdue.
  - Inability to see where teams are located without making dozens of phone calls.
  - Lack of structured proof when clients dispute service quality.
- **Daily Workflow**:
  - Reviews daily dispatch schedule on Calendar and Live Map.
  - Assigns high-priority emergency tasks to available teams.
  - Monitors the Live Map and Exception feed for blocked tasks or delayed visits.
  - Reviews team completion rates and resolves operational escalations.

---

### 2.4. The Field Supervisor — "Sarah Miller"
- **Role**: Team Lead / Dispatcher / Senior Supervisor
- **Primary Interface**: Web Application (Desktop / Laptop) & Mobile Tablet in Field
- **Primary Goals**:
  - Verify that field technicians perform work according to strict safety and quality standards.
  - Rapidly triage operational roadblocks (e.g., locked gates, missing parts).
  - Review and approve proof of work before jobs are closed.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Field technicians submitting blurry, unusable photos after leaving the job site.
  - Workers claiming they were on-site when they were actually miles away.
  - Spending half the day answering calls asking "What should I do next?".
- **Daily Workflow**:
  - Morning briefing with assigned field crew.
  - Live monitoring of technician arrivals via GPS check-in alerts.
  - Reviewing submitted photo proofs and checklist sign-offs in real time.
  - Approving completed tasks or re-opening tasks if proof is inadequate.

---

### 2.5. The Field Worker — "Carlos Mendez"
- **Role**: Field Technician / Installer / Service Specialist
- **Primary Interface**: Mobile Application (Android / iOS Smartphone)
- **Physical Environment**: High-glare outdoors, dusty equipment rooms, poor cellular reception, wearing work gloves, vehicle transit.
- **Primary Goals**:
  - Know exactly where to go, what tools are needed, and what tasks to perform without confusion.
  - Easily prove arrival and completion so work is credited without dispute.
  - Get through app workflows in seconds without fighting buggy forms or dead cellular zones.
- **Key Pain Points**:
  - Apps that freeze or crash when losing cell signal in basements or rural sites.
  - Complex screens with tiny touch targets that cannot be tapped with dirty hands or gloves.
  - Accidental data loss when battery drops or phone restarts.
  - Unjust accusations of being late when the office gave bad coordinates.
- **Daily Workflow**:
  - Clocks in on the mobile app upon arriving at the service depot or first site.
  - Reviews chronological visit and task list for the day.
  - Taps "Navigate" to launch GPS directions to the first customer site.
  - Checks in upon arrival (geofence validates location).
  - Follows step-by-step checklist, logs notes, attaches photos, captures client signature.
  - Checks out, marks task completed, and moves to the next visit.
